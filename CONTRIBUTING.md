# Contributing

Lichii is developed in this fork. Open issues and pull requests on
[Kerberone/Lichii](https://github.com/Kerberone/Lichii).

See the [build guide](docs/Build.md) for your platform and the
[coding guidelines](docs/Coding.md) for technical guidance. Keep changes focused,
describe what you tested, and distinguish build checks from gameplay testing.

For bug reports, share reproducible steps and redacted logs, **not** game images,
firmware, console keys, full memory/frame captures, secrets, or personal data.
Use a private channel to report accidentally exposed credentials or sensitive data
(see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)). Do not post such material in public PRs or issues.

Original copyright notices and third-party licenses remain in effect. See
[LICENSE.txt](LICENSE.txt) for the project's license. For ported changes, record
upstream links and preserve authorship and license notices.
